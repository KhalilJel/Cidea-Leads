# Commercial offer — Cidea Leads

## Positioning
Cidea Leads sells qualified sales opportunities, not contact lists.

## Entry point
**Uforpliktende vurdering** — no cost, no obligation. We review the target market and identify where relevant opportunities may exist.

## Paid offers
### Lead Pilot — 7 500 NOK
A focused pilot for one target market. We define the ICP, research relevant companies, qualify the strongest opportunities and deliver a sales-ready shortlist.

### Lead Engine — from 12 500 NOK/month
Ongoing lead generation for a defined market, with recurring research, qualification and structured delivery.

### Custom
For multiple markets, larger volumes or more complex qualification. Scope and price are agreed after the initial assessment.

## Sales flow
Assessment → target market → paid pilot → recurring lead engine.

## Rule
Do not promise meetings, revenue or conversion rates before there is real evidence. Show actual results as they are generated.
