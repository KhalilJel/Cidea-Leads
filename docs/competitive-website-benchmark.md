## Patterns to borrow, not copy
- Explain what is actually delivered: target definition, researched accounts, qualification criteria, context and next action.
- Distinguish a researched lead list from appointment setting or full outbound execution.
- Show an illustrative delivery sample and label it clearly as an example.
- Be explicit about fit, scope and what the customer must provide.
- Avoid promises about lead volume, response rates or revenue without evidence.

## Cidea decisions
Keep the dark charcoal/lime identity. Sell relevance and sales context—not just volume. Make deliverables, qualification rules, and the boundary between research and outreach unmistakable.
