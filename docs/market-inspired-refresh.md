# Market-inspired website refresh — Cidea Leads

## Benchmark-derived decisions
- Sell a clear lead-generation process, not a vague promise of "more leads".
- Explain target-market definition, research, qualification, context and next action.
- Show sample delivery as illustrative data, not as a real customer result.
- Make the offer understandable to Norwegian SMBs without enterprise jargon.

## Visual direction
- Retain the dark charcoal and acid-lime identity: it is distinctive from the other Cidea brands.
- Keep lime for primary actions and important signals; use it sparingly for hierarchy.
- Improve mobile navigation so the service pages remain reachable on small screens.
- Maintain a data-led interface without unsupported performance claims.

## Implementation sequence
1. Validate routes, service pages, CTA links and language behaviour.
2. Improve mobile navigation, focus visibility and reduced-motion support.
3. Clarify the offer, delivery example and qualification criteria.
4. Review commercial pages and lead-generation explanations.
5. Verify responsive widths and build.

## Guardrails
No production deployment, DNS/MX changes, prospect emails, fabricated client results or secrets in Git.
