# What is a qualified lead?

A qualified lead is a prospect that meets the criteria that make it relevant for sales.

That doesn't necessarily mean the person already wants to buy. It means the business or person fits the profile you want to reach.

## Which criteria can be used?

It varies between businesses, but typical criteria are industry, geographic area, size, service needs and the type of customer the business wants to prioritise.

The clearer the criteria, the easier it becomes to separate relevant prospects from the rest of the market.

## Why it matters

When the sales team spends time on the right companies from the start, prospecting becomes more effective and less random.
