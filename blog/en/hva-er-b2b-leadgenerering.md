# What is B2B lead generation?

B2B lead generation is about finding companies that may need what you offer, assessing which ones actually fit your target market and making it easier for sales to get in touch.

## Why does qualification matter?

A big list of companies isn't necessarily valuable. Good prospects should fit criteria such as industry, size, geography, need and other factors relevant to the sale.

## From list to sales foundation

A good lead process combines data with judgement. The goal isn't the most names, but a better foundation for relevant sales conversations.

## When can B2B lead generation be useful?

It can be especially useful when the sales team spends a lot of time on research, struggles to find the right companies or wants to expand into new markets.
