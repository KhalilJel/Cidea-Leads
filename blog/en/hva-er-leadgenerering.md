# What is lead generation?

Lead generation is about finding potential customers who may need what a business offers, and getting them ready for the sales process.

## Why is quality more important than volume?

A long contact list isn't necessarily a good pipeline. Good leads fit the target market, are the right type of business and could genuinely have a relevant need.

## A simple model

Cidea Leads uses a simple way of thinking: **Find → Qualify → Connect → Measure**.

First you find relevant companies. Then they're qualified against the criteria that matter. The prospect is prepared for sales, and the results are used to improve the process.

## Where should a business start?

First define who you actually want as a customer. Industry, size, geography and other criteria make it possible to search more precisely.

From there you can build a smaller, better target market instead of collecting as many names as possible.
